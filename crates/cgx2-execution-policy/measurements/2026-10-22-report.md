For the selected 1,048,576 element, four-worker profile, observed sequential
costs with a 10% high-side margin give `[439225, 505424]` ns and parallel costs
give `[289794, 364930]` ns. Parallel upper is below sequential lower. The
held-out median parallel speedup was about 1.48×. The profile command printed
`ParallelCpu`, `MeasuredParallelBenefit`, calibrated cost status, and result
hash `98ed6a86e558d6e0`. Out-of-range sizes fall back to sequential.

The 8-worker 1,048,576 element median was faster, but one phase had more than
2× within-phase variation, so this policy makes no selection from it. The
2-worker profile showed five held-out regressions. These results are sensitive
to contention, clock and thermal state; five held-out repetitions do not
establish a durable latency distribution. The profile expires after 24 hours,
and the committed raw CSV is review evidence rather than an active profile.
